The workshop's description · 30 December 2024

Dear friends,

Three years ago we launched this page, dedicated to the icons on glass painted in our workshop.
The first icon we showed you that day was the icon of Saint Nektarios, made for a holy Church in Măcin.

Today, three years later, we are showing you another icon of Saint Nektarios, also made for a Church.
This time, from Arad.

Thank you for all the orders we have received. Thank you also for your support, for liking our posts, and for your comments and for sharing the posts with your friends and in groups.

Today, on our third anniversary, we wish you and your families a new year of achievements, health, peace and joy, and for our workshop, many more beautiful icons painted on glass.

Happy New Year 2025!
