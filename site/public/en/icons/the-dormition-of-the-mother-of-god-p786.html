The Dormition of the Mother of God

First shown on 15 August 2022

ThemeThe Mother of God
Feast day15 August
Painted forA church in Dobruja

The workshop's description · 14 August 2025

«The Dormition of the Mother of God» – 15 August

• We welcome the great Feast of «the Dormition of the Mother of God» with an icon painted on glass in our workshop for a holy Church.

• «The icon of «the Dormition of the Mother of God» has a rich and deep spiritual meaning, revealing for each of us. In it we can see the falling asleep, the burial, the resurrection and the ascension of the Mother of God, steps through which we too may pass. The icon of the scene of «the Dormition of the Mother of God» describes a lofty state of reverence of the whole Universe towards the All-holy Theotokos.» (Fr. Nicolai Boian)
