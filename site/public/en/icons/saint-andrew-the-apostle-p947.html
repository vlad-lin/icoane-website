The workshop's description · 29 November 2023

⚜️ 30 November: «Saint Andrew the Apostle»

◘ Father Cleopa: “Saint Andrew the Apostle brought us to Christianity and celebrated the first Holy Liturgy on Romanian soil. He freed us from demons and passions and united us with Christ through sacrifice and the Liturgy. He taught us how to be saved through humility, through prayer and through love...”

◘ The icon we are showing you today (painted on glass, 22x26 cm) was ordered as a gift for a special person celebrating their name day today!

◘ “Happy name day, with faith and love” to the friends of this page who bear the name of Saint Andrew!
