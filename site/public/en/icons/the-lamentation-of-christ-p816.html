The workshop's description · 9 April 2026

⊹ Good Friday, silence and prayer...

⊹ “Let us kneel with great faith at the Tomb of the Lord, let us pray and ask forgiveness, let us be reconciled with one another, and on the holy night of Easter let us await the mysterious moment of the Resurrection of the Lord and of our own resurrection” (Father Cleopa)

⊹ On Good Friday we are showing you an icon on glass, «The Lamentation of Christ» – model: Șcheii Brașovului, 19th century.

⊹ About the icon:

• The theme of this icon is «the Lamentation of Jesus», after the Descent from the Cross and before the burial itself.

• The icon is one of the rare compositions developed horizontally, as is proper to the epitaphios.

• Its central scene shows the laying of Jesus Christ in the tomb: two apostles hold the shroud at His head and feet, and two angels lift Him from the front. In the background, Mary is surrounded by the holy women and by archangels; seraphim float in the air and (above the cross) the "thrones", angels set in medallions. In the corners of the scene the four Evangelists are shown in medallions. In the upper register, between the medallions and the arch of the vault, two landscapes can be seen: Mount Golgotha on the right and the city of Jerusalem on the left.

⊹ The technique is the traditional one: egg tempera and gold leaf.

⊹ The icon is in the collection of a lovely family from Brașov, so it is not available. If you would like an icon on this theme or any other iconographic theme, contact me and we will settle the details.
