The workshop's description

Icon on glass, ”Jesus Christ, King and Great High Priest, flanked by archangels”. Model: workshop in Țara Bârsei, second half of the 19th century
DESCRIPTION
Pen-and-ink drawing on glass, fixed with varnish. Painting: tempera, egg emulsion and linseed oil; final protective layer (on the back): oil colours and linseed oil. The icon is painted on glass, 21 x 29 cm. Stained fir frame.
DETAILS
The icon is painted “in mirror image” (partly reconstructed) after a model from Țara Bârsei, second half of the 19th century.
The icon is not available; it will be painted to order.

Why Christ is High Priest, Teacher and King (source: “40 Orthodox theses about God and the salvation of man” – the newspaper Răsunetul)
Jesus Christ is the supreme Teacher or Prophet by His very Person; as a Teacher He is not like any other man, and as a Prophet He surpasses all who came before Him, because He stands on another plane. His teaching is unique, because He is unique, and He is one with His teaching, which springs from Him. “Learn from Me, for I am meek and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.”
Jesus Christ is the supreme prophecy and the culminating revelation, and here we find what is to be known about God and man in all its fullness. The word of Jesus Christ is the living truth and is identical with His Person, for He became the hypostasis of the human spirit. The Person of Jesus Christ and the Kingdom of God that He preaches, preaching Himself, are an absolute and final newness; here lies the infinity of life, this Kingdom of God being the one and only chance of eternal life.
Jesus Christ speaks to people as God, and also as man. He communicates to them the Word of God, but He also communicates His answer as man to God, for He prays for them and teaches them to pray. His teaching calls us to answer Him, while at the same time it glorifies God. As High Priest, Jesus Christ offers Himself as a sacrifice, and this ministry is directed straight to God the Father, but it is also directed towards us, because He wishes to include us in it. Jesus Christ offers as a sacrifice the human nature He took on, so this high-priestly ministry also has its effect on the human nature He assumed. Christ's sacrifice was made for the restoration of human nature in Him, for the deification of this nature in Him. Christ's sacrifice restores communion between God and man. That is why Jesus Christ is always in a state of sacrifice, giving Himself wholly to the Father while at the same time having compassion on us. That is why He is the supreme High Priest, because He is also God; that is why Christ intercedes for us with the Father. Man wishes to enter into communion with God, and God accepts him because man wishes and strives not to sin any more; so God's acceptance comes not because man suffers a punishment for his sin, but, as I said before, because of his sincere efforts to leave the realm of sin.
Jesus Christ is King, and the Saviour is raised to this full royal power through His Resurrection, His Ascension and His sitting at the right hand of the Father, from where He guides the work of salvation and from where He will come in great glory to judge the world. The Saviour's Resurrection shows and proves that history is destined to be raised to another plane, higher than its present one: the plane of incorruptible life, where there is no death, where the freedom of the human spirit, through the Holy Spirit, makes the body transparent.
