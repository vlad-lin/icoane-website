The workshop's description · 25 October 2024

• “Share out your earthly wealth... Let us seek the heavenly one.” (Saint Demetrius)

• 26 October: the feast of the holy Great Martyr Demetrius the Myrrh-streaming (Dumitru), one of the great holy martyrs, a wonderworking and healing saint.

• We welcome this great Feast with an icon on glass painted in our workshop (model: Mărginimea Sibiului, second half of the 19th century)

• Happy name day to all who celebrate!🍂

Saint Demetrius the Great Martyr

The icon of the saint that I showed you a month ago no longer exists. Somewhere on its way between Bucharest and Arad, the Romanian Post turned it into shards
