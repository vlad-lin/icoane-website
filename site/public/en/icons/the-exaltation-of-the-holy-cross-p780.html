The workshop's description

✠ 14 September – «The Exaltation of the Holy Cross»

• «The Exaltation of the Holy Cross» is the oldest and most important of the Christian feasts dedicated to honouring the Cross of Jesus.

• Tomorrow's feast is a symbol of Christ's victory over death and sin, and stands for hope and salvation for all the faithful.

• The icon I am showing you today, [on the day of the great feast of «the Exaltation of the Holy Cross»], depicts «Saints Constantine and Helen», in the traditional model of the two emperors standing on either side of the Holy Cross.
