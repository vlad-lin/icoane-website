The workshop's description · 19 May 2026

• 21 May – «The Ascension of the Lord» – icon on glass (model: Nicula, early 19th century)

• The scene shows «the Ascension of Christ», the moment when the Saviour rises into heaven in glory, while the apostles and the Mother of God look on in wonder and faith.

• “The Ascension of Christ in glory and His sitting at the right hand of the Father is the image of the complete deification of His humanity. Through all His acts – incarnation, death, resurrection – He gradually deified the human nature He had taken on, but through the Ascension He transfigured it completely. Thanks to the supreme transfiguration of His body, Christ can dwell within those who believe in Him. The Ascension of the Lord does not mean His withdrawal from creation, for He continues to be present and at work through the Holy Spirit. The bodily Ascension into heaven is a testimony that man was created for eternity, for the Son presents Himself to the Father not only as God, but also as Man.” (Fr. Florin Vânătoru)🙏
