13 May 2023

◘ 14 May: the National Day of Remembrance of the martyrs of the communist prisons
◘ The icon “The Mother of God with the Saints of the Prisons”, in the ossuary of the “Exaltation of the Holy Cross” Hermitage in Aiud, inspired the icon painted on glass that I am showing you today
◘ A few words about two of the places where Romanian martyrs were tortured by the communist secret police:
• Râpa Robilor (the Slaves' Ravine) is the place where, with no graves and no crosses, the tortured bodies of the confessors of Aiud prison were thrown by the communist torturers, so that they could never be identified.
On this ground, sanctified by the blood of Romanian martyrs, stands the monument “The Calvary of Aiud” (a temple of suffering and pain), raised in memory of the political prisoners of the communist persecution of 1947–1964
• The former prison in Pitești, known as “the Pitești Experiment”, is the place where, through beatings, humiliation and brutal torture inflicted daily in order to “totally re-educate” the political prisoners (mostly students, intellectuals and members of political parties), the prisoners in fact broke down mentally or died. The essence of the method used at Pitești was to turn victims into executioners, torture being regarded as a mere means, not an end. Everything that happened at Pitești was so horrifying that it goes beyond the limits of imagination... A survivor of “the Pitești experiment” told me about it (everything that happened there, and in other prisons where he was interrogated and tortured); despite the terrifying torture he endured, he managed to remain dignified, whole and upright
• Other places where Romanians were tortured were marked by the use of torture to convert them to communist ideology. These were at Suceava, Pitești, Gherla, Târgu Ocna, Târgșor, Brașov and Ocnele Mari. The prisons for exterminating the political and intellectual elite: Sighet, Râmnicu Sărat, Galați, Aiud, Craiova, Brașov, Oradea, Pitești. Labour camps: the Danube–Black Sea Canal (Peninsula, Poarta Albă, Salcia, Periprava, Constanța, Midia, Capul Midia, Cernavodă, etc.). Interrogation prisons: Rahova, Malmaison, Uranus
