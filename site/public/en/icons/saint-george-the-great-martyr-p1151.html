The workshop's description

~ Saint George the Martyr – icon painted on glass ~
The theme of this icon is Saint George the Great Martyr, in a special iconographic depiction (model: Țara Oltului, first half of the 19th century).

This Saint was a Roman soldier of Greek origin and an officer in the Guard of the Roman emperor Diocletian. He was sentenced to death for refusing to renounce the Christian faith. As a Christian martyr he later became one of the most venerated saints of Christianity, and was especially venerated by the crusaders. Saint George is remembered by almost all the traditional Churches, usually on 23 April. He is considered the patron of many countries, regions and cities all over the world.

Stages of the work

Ink drawing · 31 January 2022

How an icon on glass is made... The pen-and-ink drawing, the painting in egg-emulsion tempera and the gold leaf on the haloes are all done on the "back of the glass". Then the painting is protected with a backing board, framed, and looked at (with admiration) from the "front of the glass".🎨
