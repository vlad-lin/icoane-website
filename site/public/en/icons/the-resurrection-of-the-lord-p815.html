The workshop's description

«The Resurrection of the Lord Jesus» (a study after a 19th-century icon, Savu Moga)

⊹The theme, treated in the Western manner, shows Jesus holding the banner of victory, standing on the clouds. The soldiers who guarded His tomb look up at Him. The two Myrrh-bearing Women complete the picture, together with a praying angel. The soldiers wear hussar uniforms: red and green tunics with gold braid, blue trousers, red boots and helmets with red crowns, gilded mounts and plumes. The soldier on the left wears a shirt of gilded chain mail and holds a curved sabre above his head. The composition is set within a border. (Description: the National Museum of the Romanian Peasant)

⊹The icon is not available. If you would like an icon on glass on this theme (or any other iconographic theme), contact me and we will settle the details.

⊹May the Feast of the Resurrection be blessed with joy in every soul and in every home!
