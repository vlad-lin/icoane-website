The workshop's description · 5 December 2023

⚜️Saint Nicholas the Hierarch – 6 December

✧ “Saint Nicholas was gentle, without malice and humble in spirit, guarding himself against pride. To the poor he was a father; to the needy, merciful; a comforter to those who wept; a helper to the wronged, and a great benefactor to all” (doxologia.ro)

✧ We welcome the great feast with an icon on glass (a study after an icon from 1887, Arpașu de Sus, Savu Moga), which is in the collection of a lovely Romanian family in Transylvania, together with other icons painted in our workshop

✧ Happy name day to all who celebrate!🌼
