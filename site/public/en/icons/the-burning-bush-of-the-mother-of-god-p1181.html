The workshop's description

The Burning Bush – icon painted on glass

"The Burning Bush is the symbol of unceasing prayer: whoever prays unceasingly is like this bush that burned and was not consumed. We are always under the fire of God – this blaze of light and power – and the more it burns, the brighter we become and the closer to God. That is one meaning of the Burning Bush."
Father Sofian Boghiu

Under the name “The Burning Bush of the Mother of God”, a Christian and cultural society was active at the Antim Monastery in Bucharest (between 1946 and 1948) which regarded prayer as one of the paths to salvation. The initiator and driving force of this society was the poet and journalist Sandu Tudor, who became a monk under the name Daniil. Together with a group of lay intellectuals and monks (a group that wished to preserve the true Orthodox faith and Orthodox values in times of atheist communism), he founded a Christian and cultural society that regarded prayer as one of the paths to salvation. The association's name was “The Burning Bush” – the bush which, according to the Bible, burns with an ever-green flame and which symbolises the union of the saved soul with the absolute. Sandu Tudor brought an old icon from Mount Athos dedicated to the Burning Bush, an icon from which I dared to take inspiration in painting the icon I am showing you today. The icon is not available; I will paint it again only to order.
