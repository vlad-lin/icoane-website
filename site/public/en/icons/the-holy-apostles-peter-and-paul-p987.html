The Holy Apostles Peter and Paul

Published on 28 June 2023

ThemeSaints and archangels
Feast day29 June

The workshop's description

• 29 June: «The Holy Apostles Peter and Paul» – teachers of faith, repentance and the Church's mission in the world
• Happy name day to all who celebrate!
• In Orthodox iconography (and so also in the icon painted on glass in our workshop, which we are showing you today) the two apostles are painted together: Peter holding a bunch of keys and Paul a sword. What is the meaning of these two elements, what do these objects signify?
• Fr. Constantin Sturzu explains: «The keys that Saint Peter holds are “the keys of the Kingdom of Heaven”; they unlock the gates of Paradise, but only through the sacrament of repentance, or confession. The sword means the diligent preaching of the word of God: “For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and spirit, of joints and marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart" (Hebrews 4:12). So if we receive the word of God, even if it stings at first like a cut from a sword, it then opens the gates of eternity to us, just as a key opens the door of a house»
