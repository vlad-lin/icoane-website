The workshop's description

✤ 9 June 2025 – The Holy Trinity ✤

• Fr. Teofil Părăian: "We believe that God is One in being and threefold in person. It is a mystery of our faith, perhaps the greatest mystery, something we cannot understand but can receive through faith. This is the divine revelation."

• On the Monday after Pentecost Sunday (which is dedicated to glorifying the Holy Trinity), we are showing you the icon painted on glass in our workshop: «The Holy Trinity and the Coronation of the Mother of God» (model: workshop in Șcheii Brașovului, 19th century)

• The scene shows the Holy Trinity (the spiritual unity of the three divine persons): Jesus Christ carrying the Cross of the crucifixion, God the Father with the orb and sceptre (the insignia of the Almighty), and the Holy Spirit (as a white dove framed by the sun) sending down His blessing. The first two hold a crown above the head of the Virgin Mary (kneeling, dressed in a red maphorion, with her hands crossed on her chest). The figures are ”held up” by clouds.

• This icon was made to order, so it is not available. If you would like a similar icon, contact me.
