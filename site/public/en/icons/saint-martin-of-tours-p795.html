✨️Saint Martin of Tours (a scene from his life) ~ Icon painted on glass

• Saint Martin is celebrated on 11 November and on 4 July (the date he was raised to bishop, in 371).

• A short biography of an ascetic hierarch:

• Saint Martin is one of the most popular saints in the Catholic Church, and he is also venerated as a saint in the Orthodox Church.
Saint Martin was born around the year 316 in Savaria (in the province of Pannonia, today Szombathely in Hungary), the son of a pagan Roman tribune, but he received a Christian education in Pavia, his father's home town, becoming a catechumen at the age of ten.
Obeying his father, he had to enlist in the Roman imperial army, serving in the bodyguard of Emperor Constantius II. Posted to the cavalry legions at Amiens (in Gaul), he once met a nearly naked beggar at the city gate. Moved with pity, he cut his cloak (called “cappa” in Latin) in two with his sword and gave half of it to the man in need. The following night he dreamt of the Lord Christ, who revealed to him that He Himself had been present in the form of the poor man.
