The workshop's description

Jesus – the True Vine (model: Șcheii Brașovului, early 19th century) – icon painted on glass
~The iconographic scene shows the risen Jesus Christ before the Cross (standing on the Holy Table of the Altar), and from His side grows a vine (full of grapes), which Christ's hand presses straight into the Holy Chalice. From the image one can easily deduce that the icon does not aim to highlight the metaphor of the vine, frequent in the New Testament writings, but what we find in the Holy Chalice, namely: "in the form of the wine flowing from the grapes is the very Blood of Christ (truly), for the vine grows and is nourished from His side. And on the Holy Table of the Altar lies the very body of the risen Christ".~
