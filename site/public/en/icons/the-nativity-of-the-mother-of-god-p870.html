The workshop's description · 7 September 2024

The Akathist of the Nativity of the Mother of God:
“Today the door of life is opened, today the ray of the spiritual Sun rises, today the Mother of joy is born for the salvation of the whole world.
Be glad, you peoples, and rejoice, and with faith praise her from your hearts thus: Rejoice, divine Child Mary, who today was born for the joy and salvation of the whole world!”

The icon on this theme (model: Nicula, early 19th century) that we are showing you today was painted for a holy and beautiful Orthodox Church in Dobrogea.

About the icon (National Institute of Heritage):
"Lying on a bed above which hangs a rich curtain, Saint Anne has the baby girl at her feet. Joachim urges her to eat from the dishes he brings. A border with the rope motif surrounds the composition. The novelty of the image lies in the way the floor is drawn, like a roof of curved tiles, which focuses all attention on Joachim's gesture."
