The workshop's description · 13 September 2023

❈ 14 September | The Feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross

✧ Răzvan Codrescu: “By its vertical, the cross signifies the bond between heaven and earth, the aspiration to higher things, the uprightness of a life illumined by faith. Its horizontal signifies the all-embracing embrace of the world through sacrificial love, the calling and gathering of all around the holy axis that leads to Heaven. The point where they cross, where the horizontal and the vertical “commune”, signifies the essential centre of our existence, our right place in the mystery of salvation, which is possible through man's co-working in grace with God, through the joining of matter to spirit and of the created spirit (the human soul) to the uncreated Spirit (the Holy Spirit).”

✧ The icon (model: Nicula, mid-19th century) that we are showing you in honour of the great feast was given to a holy place of worship built in memory of the martyrs of the Romanian communist prisons
