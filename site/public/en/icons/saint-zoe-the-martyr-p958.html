Saint Zoe the Martyr – icon on glass (22x26 cm)

The icon was ordered as a gift for a special lady who celebrates both her birthday and her name day on 18 December

To know part of the life of Saint Zoe, we must also know Saint Sebastian, both being celebrated by the Orthodox Church on 18 December

I found two very different versions of this story. I thought the Romanian Patriarchate was the most reliable source, so I am sharing a short text signed by the Romanian Patriarchate – Pilgrimages department (basilica.ro)

“ Saint Sebastian the Martyr was born in 255 in the city of Narbonne in Gaul (today's France), but grew up in Mediolanum (Milan, Italy), where his family came from. In 283, as the son of a nobleman, he had to join the army of Emperor Carinus (283–285). Sebastian's wish was to help the Christians in their sufferings and troubles.

The opportunity came when two brothers, Marcellinus and Mark, had confessed Christ with great courage and were quietly awaiting death. Their parents, Tranquillinus and Marcia, had asked the judges for 30 days to bring them back to paganism. At this moment of great trial, Sebastian, commander of the first cohort of the Praetorian Guard, came to them. Those present were amazed to hear Sebastian encouraging the two brothers to die for their faith in God: “Soldiers of Christ, see how, through the lifting up of your souls, you have drawn near to victory. Do not deprive yourselves of Him for such useless lamentation… If your parents and brothers knew of eternal life, so full of joy, they would hasten to go with you”

All this happened in the house of Nicostratus, who was in charge of guarding the prisoners. Zoe, the wife of Nicostratus, had been mute for six years. Seeing Sebastian encouraging the two martyrs, she fell on her knees before him, showing him that she was mute. Sebastian made the sign of the cross over her mouth, and at that moment she regained her voice. After this miracle, the martyrs Mark and Marcellinus and their parents Marcia and Tranquillinus, Zoe and her husband Nicostratus, and 16 more of the prisoners received holy baptism from the hands of the presbyter Polycarp

When the persecution of the Christians intensified, Zoe was caught while praying at the tomb of the Holy Apostle Peter and, after five days in prison, suffered a martyr's death. Marcellinus and Mark were tortured and finally received a martyr's death. Saint Sebastian the Martyr died in the hippodrome (the place where horsemen raced), entrusting his soul to God as a martyr”
