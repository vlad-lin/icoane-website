The workshop's description · 19 July 2023

On 20 July the Romanian Orthodox Church celebrates the ascent into heaven of the Holy Great Prophet Elijah the Tishbite, a saint honoured as a great wonderworker and bringer of rain in times of drought

Depicted in icons either at the mouth of his cave (which can still be visited today, in the land of Choziba) with the ravens sent to feed him, or in the chariot of fire in which he was taken up to heaven, or in both (as in the icon shown today), the Holy Great Prophet Elijah the Tishbite is the Old Testament figure dearest and most familiar to the Christian conscience everywhere

We welcome the great feast with an icon on glass made after the icon «The Ascent of the Holy Prophet Elijah into Heaven, with scenes from his life» (Șcheii Brașovului, early 19th century), in the collection of the Leon Sculy Logothetti family of Iași

Happy name day to all who celebrate!🌺
