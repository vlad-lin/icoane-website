The workshop's description

“The Burning Bush of the Theotokos”

• This icon painted on glass is inspired by several depictions, one of them being the first such icon to reach our country (in the 1940s), at the Antim Monastery in Bucharest. Many generations of the Church's servants and of the faithful have prayed before this icon at the Antim Monastery, and it also inspired the well-known hesychast movement “The Burning Bush”, a group that kept the fire of faith alive in the hearts of many Romanians during the communist rule.

• The icon “The Burning Bush” shows a large eight-pointed star in the form of overlapping rhombuses, with the Mother of God holding the Saviour in her arms at their centre. Four of the eight points are red, representing the fire, and the other four, painted green, symbolise that the bush did not burn but remained whole. On the points of the red star are the symbols of the four Holy Evangelists: the angel (Saint Matthew), the lion (Saint Mark), the ox (Saint Luke) and the eagle (Saint John), and on the four points of the green star the Holy Angels are depicted.

• This icon is not available; it was sent to Paris, with great joy.❤
