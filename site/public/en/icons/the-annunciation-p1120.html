The workshop's description · 24 March 2024

✤ 25 March – The Annunciation ✤

Father Cleopa: «Who can understand what depth of humility there was in the heart of the All-holy Virgin Mary, who, hearing and understanding from the Archangel Gabriel that she would conceive of the Holy Spirit and give birth to the Son of God, did not exalt herself in her heart, but with great humility, considering herself a simple handmaid, said: “Behold the handmaid of the Lord. Let it be to me according to your word.”»

The icon (painted on glass) shows the scene of the Annunciation to the Mother of God by the Archangel Gabriel, announcing to her that she would give birth, as a man, to the Son of God.

This small icon (model: Șcheii Brașovului, 19th century) is not available; every day it lights up the icons being painted in our workshop, which came into being five years ago, on the day of the Annunciation.

16 March 2023

~ The Annunciation – icon painted on glass (model: Șcheii Brașovului, 19th century) ~
Because I look forward with emotion to the day of "the Annunciation", because on this DAY (3 years ago) I began to paint icons on glass, because I am grateful... I am showing you an icon (on this theme) painted recently.

The theme of this icon (model: Șcheii Brașovului, mid-19th century) is the Annunciation (celebrated by the Orthodox Church every year on 25 March), the day on which the Holy Archangel Gabriel brings to Earth the news of the Birth of the Son of God. He comes to a town in Galilee (called Nazareth), to a virgin betrothed to a man (named Joseph), announcing to her that she will give birth to the Messiah. The virgin's name was Mary – the Holy Virgin Mary. The good news given to mankind about the incarnation of the Son of God in fact completes our personal feast, our own annunciation.
