The workshop's description

✨️2 February – The Presentation of the Lord✨️
• “Where is God? Wherever He is let in...” (Father Nicolae Steinhardt)
• On this great feast day I am showing you an icon with a theme that is rare in Romanian iconography (the scene of the Presentation of the Lord in the Temple), an icon painted in our workshop three years ago (model: Nicula, second half of the 18th century)
• On 2 February (40 days after His birth), the Saviour's parents performed the ritual of bringing Him to the Temple to redeem Him, as the Law of Moses required. According to the Law, the Israelites were required to bring every firstborn male to the temple and dedicate him to the Lord, a practice explained by the sparing of the Israelites' firstborn on the night they left Egypt
• The icon is honoured and sanctified by the very sacred place where it stands, thanks to a lovely Romanian family
