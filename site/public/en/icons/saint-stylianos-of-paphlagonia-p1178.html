13 January 2022

Saint Stylianos of Paphlagonia
The Church celebrates Saint STYLIANOS OF PAPHLAGONIA on 26 November. He was born in the 6th century, in Adrianople. His family was very rich, but he gave up all his wealth and became a monk. So Saint Stylianos went into the wilderness, to prayer and fasting. An angel brought him food every day in the cave where he lived. After a while, a merciless disease began to kill the children of the people of that land. The people turned to Saint STYLIANOS to intercede with God. And so the children of many were healed, and those whose babies had already died were soon able to have others. For this reason Saint STYLIANOS is considered the protector of children, especially of infants, and a great help to parents of sick children. In iconography he is shown holding a baby in his arms.
