The workshop's description · 20 October 2023

❈ On 21 October the Orthodox Church celebrates the Holy Transylvanian Confessors

• We welcome the feast of the Holy Confessors with an icon on glass (30x42 cm) painted in our workshop. The saints depicted in this icon are:

• Visarion Sarai – an Orthodox hieromonk who was imprisoned in the fortress of Kufstein, where he died confessing Orthodoxy

• Sofronie of Cioara – a Romanian monk who led the second anti-Catholic revolt in Transylvania

• Oprea Miclăuș – a Romanian peasant who travelled three times to the imperial Court in Vienna to present the Romanians' wish to remain in the faith of their forefathers. In 1752, together with the priest Moise Măcinic of Sibiel, he was imprisoned at Kufstein, where both departed to the Lord

• Ioan of Galeș – a confessor priest held responsible (by the Habsburg authorities in Transylvania) for coordinating a resistance movement against the Union with Rome; he was finally imprisoned at Kufstein, where he endured 24 years of detention

• Moise Măcinic of Sibiel – a confessor priest who fought against the imposition of the Greek Catholic confession; imprisoned at Kufstein

• The client wished us to depict in this icon also Atanasie Todoran of Bichigiu (celebrated on 12 November), sentenced to death by the imperial power for refusing to renounce the faith of his forefathers, for which he was broken on the wheel at the age of 104

• Source: “The History of the Romanian Orthodox Church” by Rev. Prof. Dr. Mircea Păcurariu

• The icon was painted to order for a lovely Romanian family from Transylvania

Stages of the work

Ink drawing · 6 March 2022

Six saints... They were worthy defenders of Orthodoxy during the Catholic oppression in the Romanian lands beyond the mountains (in the 18th century):
the Venerable Confessors Visarion, Sofronie and Atanasie of Bichigiu; the Holy Martyr Oprea; the Holy Confessor Priests Ioan of Galeș and Moise Măcinic of Sibiel.
The first stage in making an icon on glass is the pen-and-ink drawing (on the back of the glass); then comes the painting (also on the “back” of the glass), and then the framing.
It is a large icon (37 x 49 cm), very different from the usual themes of icons painted on glass, and probably the first icon on glass to take on this theme (ordered by a lovely Romanian family).

28 March 2022

~ The Holy Confessors of Transylvania, Martyrs of the true faith ~

> Two priests, two monks and two free peasants...
Six holy Transylvanian confessors, martyrs of the true faith of their forefathers, in an icon painted on glass: saints in whom the whole Orthodox Church of Transylvania is embodied – hierarchs and clergy, monks and hermits, the faithful...

> The Orthodox Church of Transylvania was (over the centuries) a martyr Church. Countless were the wrongs done by the masters of Transylvania (from the 18th century on) to the Transylvanian Church (including its servants and its faithful), but many too were the valiant fighters who defended the Orthodox faith of their forefathers against the oppression of the imperial power in Vienna. These fighters were: Oprea Miclăuș (a peasant from Săliște, near Sibiu), who suffered death for the true faith; the hieromonks Visarion Sarai and Sofronie of Cioara (near Orăștie); the priests Moise Măcinic of Sibiel and Ioan of Galeș (all five celebrated on 21 October); Atanasie Todoran of Bichigiu, in Bistrița-Năsăud (celebrated on 12 November); and many, many other men, women and children.
> The icon was painted to order, so it is not available. It can be made again, to order.
