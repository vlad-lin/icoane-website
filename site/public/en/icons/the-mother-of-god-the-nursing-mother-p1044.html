The workshop's description · 1 July 2023

◘ The icon of the Mother of God “the Nursing Mother” (Galaktotrophousa) is celebrated twice a year: 2 July and 12 January
◘ Over time, this icon has been made on wood or on glass, in various versions. One of the oldest such depictions of the Mother of God is on the Holy Mountain of Athos (Hilandar Monastery); other depictions of this theme (as miniatures) can be admired in manuscripts and (of course) many delightful depictions are found in the icons on glass of the Transylvanian icon painters, one of them (workshop in Șcheii Brașovului, second half of the 19th century) being the model after which we made this icon in our workshop
◘ The icon is not available; contact me if you would like an icon on this theme

Other presentations of this icon

11 January 2023

⚜️ 12 January: the veneration of the icon of the Mother of God “the Nursing Mother” (Galaktotrophousa)
◘ The Galaktotrophousa icon (the Nursing Mother, the Nourisher of Life) shows the fully human nature of the Saviour without overlooking the divine one... It also shows unfathomable humility: the Child Saviour receives food like any other child
◘ The icon I am showing you today (model: workshop in Șcheii Brașovului, second half of the 19th century) shows the Mother of God holding the Child Jesus in His mature aspect (Emmanuel). Jesus Christ Emmanuel is shown as a child with some features of a grown man, such as the broad forehead, which reflects His divine wisdom
◘ The icon, painted on glass (22x26 cm) in the traditional technique (egg-emulsion tempera and gold leaf), is not available; it was made to order
