The workshop's description · 18 September 2024

✧ 2024, the Year of Avram Iancu: Avram Iancu, celebrated 200 years after his birth and 152 years after his death.

• Avram Iancu, the future national hero, was born in 1824. The exact date of his birth was not recorded, but according to the hero himself he was born "in cherry time". Since cherries ripen later in the mountains because of the harsher climate, he may have been born in July or August.

• The work I am showing you today depicts Avram Iancu – “the Little Prince of the Mountains” – with Crișan, Cloșca and Horea beside Horea's Oak.
This painting on glass is inspired by a mural in the Orthodox church of “the Dormition of the Mother of God” at the Țebea Memorial Complex

•The Testament of Avram Iancu
“My last will!
The only longing of my life is to see my Nation happy, for which I have worked as far as I could until now, sadly without much success; indeed, just now I see with sorrow that my hopes and the sacrifice I made are turning to nothing.
I do not know how many days I may have left; a kind of foreboding seems to tell me that the future is uncertain. I therefore wish and firmly decree that after my death all my property, movable and immovable, shall pass to the benefit of the Nation, to help found an academy of law, for I firmly believe that those who fight with the weapon of the law will be able to win the rights of my Nation. "
