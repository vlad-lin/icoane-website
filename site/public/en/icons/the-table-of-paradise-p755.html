The workshop's description · 18 March 2026

▫︎ «The Table of Paradise» – a personalised icon

▫︎ The icon «The Table of Paradise» is divided by diagonal bands into five fields showing: the Mother of God with the Child, Christ Pantocrator, Saint Paraskeva, Saint Nicholas and, often, a floral element at the centre of the composition, symbolising Paradise.

▫︎ For a personalised icon, on request we replace Saint Paraskeva and Saint Nicholas with the saints you prefer. In today's icon, the saints of «the Table of Paradise» are Saint Eugenia and Saint Claudius.

▫︎ Saint Claudius the Martyr – 19 March

▫︎ Happy name day to all who celebrate!
