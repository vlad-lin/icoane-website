31 March 2022

~ ” Jesus Christ, King and Great High Priest, flanked by archangels ”~ Icon on glass [model: workshop in Țara Bârsei (1802)]. The icon is painted on glass (21 x 29 cm). Stained fir frame.

> The three Magi from the East (representing all mankind) came to the incarnate Lord (when He was a Child) with gifts (gold, frankincense and myrrh), whose very symbolism explains why Jesus is King and Great High Priest.
According to Fr. Ionel Popescu (Timișoara), gold was (and still is) offered to kings and emperors as a sign of esteem and respect for their high dignity. Gold is also the symbol of divinity and glory (Matthew: "the shining perfection of His divine Person"). Frankincense is brought as to a great priest, a great high priest. Jesus Christ the Lord also holds the dignity of high priest. Frankincense is an aroma or perfume and suggests the fragrance of His perfect life, wholly without sin. Frankincense (as has been known since antiquity) was used in religious services, in the temple in Jerusalem and in the synagogues. It shows that the Lord Jesus came into the world to serve and not to be served. Myrrh is a bitter plant, which foreshadows the sufferings Christ was to endure, bearing the sins of the world. Myrrh was used in burial rites: the dead were embalmed with myrrh and other spices before being laid in the tomb. The myrrh brought by the Magi symbolises the death and sacrifice of the Lord Jesus for all mankind.
