The workshop's description

«The Coronation of the Mother of God» is an iconographic theme found very often in the wall paintings of Orthodox churches, in icons on glass and in the religious paintings of the great Western painters of the Middle Ages.

Over time, the scene of the Coronation of the Mother of God has had several versions.
In all of them the coronation takes place in heaven.

One of the first versions, promoted by Western art in the early Middle Ages, shows the Virgin Mary enthroned, crowned only by angels. Later the iconographic scene was enriched and became more complex.

The version we are showing you today is painted on glass after a model from Șcheii Brașovului, 19th century.
The icon shows the scene of the Coronation of the Virgin Mary.

Jesus Christ and God the Father sit on suggested thrones and hold the mitre they place on Mary's head.
In the upper register the Holy Spirit appears as a white dove framed by the sun, flanked on each side by two cherubim floating on clouds.
In the lower register, in the middle, Mary kneels, dressed in a red maphorion with a halo on her head and her hands crossed on her chest, with cherubim at her feet.
All the figures are held up by clouds.

The icon is not available; it has gone to Constanța together with «The Eucharistic Jesus» and «The Baptism of the Lord».
