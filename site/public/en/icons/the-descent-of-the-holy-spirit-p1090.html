The Descent of the Holy Spirit

Published on 2 June 2022

ThemeJesus Christ and the great feasts
Model (izvod)Nicula, early 19th century

The workshop's description

(†) "The Ascension of the Lord" is the Feast of the Day, and today's icon on glass (model: Nicula, early 19th century) shows this theme.
(†) Christ has ascended!
"Forty days after the Resurrection of Christ and ten days before the Descent of the Holy Spirit, we celebrate the bodily Ascension into heaven of the incarnate Son of God. The Ascension of Christ shows what man's destination is: the human nature He took on was fully transfigured; it was not dissolved in its union with the divine nature. If it becomes invisible after the Ascension, this is due to its deification. Indeed, man's purpose is not to lose himself in the world, for then the most important factor of reality would be lost.
The bodily Ascension into heaven is a testimony that man was created for eternity, for the Son presents Himself to the Father as God and also as Man.
Through His Ascension, Christ not only shows where man must arrive, but becomes the way and the power for man to reach that state. He sits on the divine throne of glory, yet He also dwells in the hearts of those who love Him. This is how we can understand the paradox: Christ is ascended, and yet on the way to ascension with each one of us..." (crestinortodox.ro)
