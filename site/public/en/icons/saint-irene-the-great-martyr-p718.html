🌿«Saint Irene of Magedon, the Great Martyr» – icon on glass

- Saint Irene is celebrated on 5 May

- Saint Irene of Magedon, the Great Martyr, lived in the 4th century and suffered for her faith in Christ.

- Her name, Irene, means "peace" in Greek.

- She is known for preaching Christianity despite persecution, and for bringing many people to the faith through her sufferings.

- Her story is one of faith, courage and perseverance.
