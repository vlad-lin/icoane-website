The workshop's description · 7 November 2024

• 8 November is the day the Church dedicates to all the angels who did not turn away from God, which is why this feast appears in the calendar as “the Synaxis of the Holy Archangels Michael and Gabriel and of all the heavenly powers”.

• As we wait for the great feast, I am showing you “the Holy Archangels Michael and Gabriel” in two icons on glass painted in our workshop.
The first is a study after an icon painted on wood by Toma Zugravul of Bărbătești, Vâlcea, in 1895, an icon kept in a holy Church that is a historic monument.
The second is a study after an icon painted on glass by Savu Moga, Arpașu de Sus, 1890.

• This feast dates from the 5th century, beginning as the simple anniversary of the consecration of the church of Saint Michael the Archangel built at the Baths of Arcadius in Constantinople. Over time, the feast came to be dedicated to all the Holy Angels. The seven angels we celebrate are:

• Michael – translated as “the power of God”. He is the commander of the heavenly hosts and the first of the Holy Archangels; he carries the fiery sword, and his role is to guard the law of God and to overcome all who do not obey it. He is also the one who stopped the angels from falling from heaven, who defended the body of Moses and who brought the ten plagues upon Pharaoh's people. It is said that at the end of the age he will raise the dead and gather all nations to judgement.

• Gabriel is translated as “man of God”, being the archangel of the Annunciation. He carries no fiery sword, but the lily of undefiled joy. He was the first to speak the name of Jesus, the one who brought the great mystery of the Lord's incarnation, the news of the conception, and who gave Saint John the Baptist his name.

• Raphael is the healer of human infirmities

• Uriel is the enlightener of those in darkness

• Selaphiel is the one who prays for mankind

• Jegudiel is the glorifier of God and the strengthener of those who do good works

• Barachiel is the bringer of God's blessing upon the earth

• Happy name day to all who celebrate!
