The workshop's description

17 April: «The Life-giving Spring»

⊹ A feast on Bright Friday... It is a feast dedicated to the Mother of God, recalling the role of the Virgin Mary in the salvation of mankind.

⊹ A warm feast dedicated to the Mother of God, reminding us of Good Friday, of the tears and pain of the Sorrowful Mother... Then, the Mother of God suffered and wept beside the Saviour's Cross.
Today, the tears of the Mother of God are a Healing Spring of hope and of healing from illness, wounds and suffering.

⊹ The icon we are showing you today is “The Sorrowful Mother of God”, painted in our workshop (model: Savu Moga, Țara Făgărașului, 1877).

- This work is one of the most dramatic in Savu Moga's whole body of work, thanks to the elegance, grace and expressiveness of the Mother of God's face.

- Everything stands out against a background with a reddish patina.

- Groups of cherubim whirl among the figures of the Holy Spirit, God the Father and the crucified Jesus.

- The elongated body of Jesus is fluid and graceful above Golgotha.

- In the left corner there is a Cyrillic inscription, a quatrain, very unusual for master Moga, which says: “... He who by His word alone / made the heavens and the earth / is nailed upon the cross / a thing unheard of / 1877”.
