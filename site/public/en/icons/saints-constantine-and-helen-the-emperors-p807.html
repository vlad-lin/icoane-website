❀ 21 May – Saints Constantine and Helen, the Emperors

• “By the cross the Christian conquers, just as Emperor Constantine conquered in Christ.”

• We welcome the great feast of Saints Constantine and Helen – models of faith, wisdom and devotion to Christianity – with an icon on glass painted in our workshop.

• I painted this icon on glass with gratitude and joy, for a distinguished family from Arad. It is a special work, inspired by an icon on glass from the Nicula workshop, mid-19th century.

• Happy name day to all named Constantin and Elena! May they protect you and guide you on the path of faith! 🙏✨
