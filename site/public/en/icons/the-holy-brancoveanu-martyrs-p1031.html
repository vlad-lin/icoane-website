The Holy Brâncoveanu Martyrs

First shown on 19 August 2022

ThemeMartyrs and heroes
Feast day18 February
AvailabilityMade to order
Painted forA church in western Romania

The workshop's description · 17 February 2023

◘ 18 February marks 71 years since the departure from earthly life of the pillar of fire of Christian spirituality in the communist prisons, Valeriu Gafencu (whom Nicolae Steinhardt called “the Saint of the Prisons”)
◘ Valeriu Gafencu was born on 24 January 1921 in Sângerei, Bessarabia; he was one of the young intellectuals who died in the prisons of Romania's communist regime of those times
◘ In this icon painted on glass (ordered as a gift to a holy Church in western Romania), Saint Valeriu Gafencu is shown alongside the Holy Brâncoveanu Martyrs

Stages of the work

Ink drawing · 29 July 2022

• For a holy Church ... Martyrs of our people:
• The holy Brâncoveanu martyrs – Prince Constantin with his four sons (Constantin, Ștefan, Radu, Matei) and the counsellor Ianache – defenders and keepers of the faith of our forefathers
• The holy martyr Valeriu Gafencu: “the pillar of fire of Christian spirituality in the communist prisons”, called by Nicolae Steinhardt “the Saint of the Prisons”
