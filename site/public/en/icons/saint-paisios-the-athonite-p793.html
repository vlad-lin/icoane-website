Saint Paisios the Athonite

First shown on 6 October 2022

ThemeSaints and archangels
Feast day12 July

The workshop's description · 11 July 2025

• On 12 July the Orthodox Church remembers Saint Paisios the Athonite, one of the best-known and most beloved saints of the 20th century.
Saint Paisios was born on 25 July 1924 in Farasa, Cappadocia, and departed to the Lord on 12 July 1994.

• On this special day, I am showing you an icon on glass (of Saint Paisios) made in our workshop for a young family from Maramureș.

• I am also sharing with you the Testament of the Venerable Paisios the Athonite

After the death of Elder Paisios, a precious text in the Father's own hand was found at the Panagouda cell:
“When I, the renowned Paisios, examined myself, I saw that I had broken all the commandments of the Lord and committed all the sins. It does not matter if I committed some of them to a lesser degree, for I have no extenuating circumstances at all, since I received so many blessings from the Lord. Pray that Christ may have mercy on me. Forgive me, and may all those who think they have grieved me be forgiven. Thank you very much, and again, pray! The monk Paisios”. (From Saint Paisios the Athonite – Evanghelismos Publishing)

11 July 2023

• Saint Paisios the Athonite (12 July)
• The Venerable Paisios the Athonite is one of the most beloved fathers who have lived on the Holy Mountain of Athos. He fell asleep in the Lord on 12 July 1994 and was buried in the grounds of the Monastery of Saint John the Evangelist in Souroti (near Thessaloniki)
• He is celebrated in the Orthodox Church on 12 July
• The icon of Saint Paisios (which we are showing you today) was made in our workshop for a lovely family from Maramureș

6 October 2022

“ Saint Paisios the Athonite (1924–1994) is a name that brings joy and comfort just by being spoken. After a life of ascetic struggle and of love for God and neighbour, Father Paisios the Athonite was officially numbered among the saints, with a day of remembrance (yearly feast) on 12 July” (doxologia.ro)
