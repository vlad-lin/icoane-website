18 February: the Saint of the Prisons

Valeriu Gafencu: “Do not expect happiness to come from anywhere other than from within you, where Christ dwells".

18 February marks 72 years since the departure from earthly life of the pillar of fire of Christian spirituality in the communist prisons, Valeriu Gafencu, whom Nicolae Steinhardt called “the Saint of the Prisons”.

In this icon painted on glass (22x26 cm), Saint Valeriu Gafencu is shown alongside the Holy Brâncoveanu Martyrs.
Photo: in the home of a special family from Transylvania.

Other presentations of this icon

17 February 2022

"The Saint of the Prisons" Valeriu Gafencu and the Holy Brâncoveanu Martyrs – icon painted on glass –

18 February 2022 marks 70 years since the falling asleep in the Lord of the young Valeriu Gafencu (a student at the Faculty of Law and Philosophy in Iași), one of the young Romanian intellectuals who lit up and sanctified the communist prisons with their lives. In these prisons they were tortured, humiliated and subjected to unimaginable torment for many years (at Aiud, for example), or "re-educated" by the murderous torturers of the communist extermination camps (at Pitești). Some of these martyrs miraculously survived and told us about the horrors they endured; otherwise we would never have known the whole truth. Valeriu Gafencu, however [born in Sângerei (Bălți County, in Bessarabia) on 24 January 1921], died in Târgu Ocna prison at the age of 31.

Valeriu Gafencu – the Saint of the Prisons:
"Seek happiness in your souls. Do not seek it outside yourselves. Do not expect happiness to come from anywhere other than from within you, from your soul, where the Lord of Love, Christ, dwells. If you expect happiness from outside yourselves, you will suffer disappointment after disappointment and will never reach it."
