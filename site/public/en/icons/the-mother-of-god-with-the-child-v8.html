The Mother of God with the Child

From the workshop's archive · photographed on 9 August 2019

ThemeThe Mother of God
Model (izvod)Northern Transylvania, mid-19th century
Size15 × 21 cm

The workshop's description

Specialists say that this icon painted on glass (thought to come from the mid-19th century, in northern Transylvania) is a combination of Orthodox and Catholic elements.

Icon painters of every age have tried to show the Mother of God as beautiful, gentle, dignified and glorified as possible. Sometimes they painted her saddened, sometimes sorrowful, but always full of spiritual strength and wisdom. The All-pure one is always shown with her head covered by a veil falling onto her shoulders, according to the Jewish tradition of those times. In most icons, the Mother of God holds the Child on her left arm and points to Him with her right hand; this icon, painted on anti-reflective glass, is one of the versions in which the Child is held in her right hand.

Hand-painted on glass in tempera and gold leaf, over a pen-and-ink drawing made after the original model. The stained fir frame is also handmade.
