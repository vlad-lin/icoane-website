The Presentation of the Lord – icon painted on glass

The theme of this icon (model: Nicula, second half of the 19th century) is "the Presentation in the Temple", an event closely linked to the theme of purification and redemption.
Forty days after Christmas we celebrate the Presentation of the Lord.

Jesus Christ is brought to the Temple in Jerusalem by the Holy Virgin Mary and Joseph the Righteous to fulfil the rules of the old Law (the Law of Moses), which required that, 40 days after the birth of a first child, the child be presented at the Temple in Jerusalem with an offering to redeem the firstborn, who in fact belonged to God. The offering was a lamb and a dove. The poor, who had no lamb, could bring two young pigeons (one of which symbolised the offering of thanks to God for the gift received, while the other was the price of redemption for the child, who would otherwise have had to dedicate his life to the service of God).
