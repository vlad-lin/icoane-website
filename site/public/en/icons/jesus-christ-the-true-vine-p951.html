The workshop's description

❈ «Jesus with the Vine» – icon on glass (model: Șcheii Brașovului, early 19th century)

• The theme of this icon is found in the Transylvanian peasant icons evoking the mystery of Holy Communion;

• The icon illustrates the parable of the vine: “I am the vine, you are the branches”;

• Jesus presented Himself as the true Vine, His Father as the vinedresser who planted the true vine, and His disciples as the true branches of this Vine. It is an archetype according to which the essential elements of life – for food, healing and worship – grew out of the primordial sacrifice;

• The icon is not available; if you like this extraordinary theme, contact me and we will settle the details of the order
