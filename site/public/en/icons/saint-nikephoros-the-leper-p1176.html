Doxologia.ro:
The Venerable Nikephoros the Leper was born on the island of Crete into a family of devout peasants and was orphaned at an early age. At thirteen, the first signs of the “holy disease”, as leprosy is called in the Church of Christ, appeared on his body. To escape the permanent isolation that awaited him, he took refuge for a time in Egypt. At twenty-four he arrived at the leper colony on Chios, in the care of Saint Anthimos of Chios, where he was tonsured a monk. There, for forty-one years, although more and more crippled by the disease, he gave himself with a martyr's patience to struggles beyond nature, becoming an example for his brothers in suffering.
After the cure for leprosy was discovered in 1947, the leper colony on Chios was closed and the Venerable one was moved to the Saint Barbara Hospital in Athens. Blind and confined to his sickbed, yet endowed with many divine gifts, he spent the last years of his life giving hope to the despairing, comfort to the suffering and zeal to the young. At the age of seventy-four the holy leper departed this world; later, the finding of his holy relics and the countless healings he still works today showed him to be one of the greatest intercessors of our times.
In 2012 the Ecumenical Patriarchate glorified Saint Nikephoros the Leper, with his feast on 4 January, the day of his falling asleep.
