Orders

“If you would like an icon on glass with a particular theme (or any other iconographic theme), contact me and we will settle the details.”

  • Any iconographic theme — the great feasts, the Mother of God, patron saints, after models (izvoade) from Nicula, Șcheii Brașovului, Țara Oltului, Laz or Mărginimea Sibiului.
  • Personalised icons — for example “The Table of Paradise”, where the saints in the diamonds are chosen on request.
  • Gifts with a soul — for a baptism, a name day or an anniversary, with the patron saint of the person receiving it.
  • For churches and monasteries — many of the works are already in churches across Romania.
  • Usual sizes — from small 13 × 18 cm icons up to 30 × 42 cm or larger.

Most of the works in the gallery were made to order and are no longer available, but any theme can be painted again. Orders are scheduled in the order they arrive.
