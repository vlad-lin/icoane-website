Who we are

This website, icoane.biz, belongs to the Byzantine Workshop of Icons on Glass (Atelierul Bizantin de Icoane pe Sticlă). For any question about your data, write to us at contact@icoane.biz.

What data we collect

The site has no user accounts, forms or newsletter, and does not ask for personal data.

If you accept statistics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to learn, in aggregate, how many people visit the site, which pages they read, their country and approximate city, their type of device and browser, and which site brought them here. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. We do not use this data for advertising and do not combine it with other data.

If you write to us by e-mail, Messenger or WhatsApp, we use your message only to reply to you and for your order.

Cookies

Only after you press “Accept” does Google Analytics store cookies whose names start with _ga in your browser, to tell new visits from returning ones. They expire after at most 2 years. If you decline, no statistics cookie is stored.

Your choice is kept in your browser so that we do not ask on every page.

Legal basis and withdrawing consent

Statistics are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

Who processes the data

Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Limited. Data may be transferred to the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Detailed statistics data is kept for 2 months. Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web server, may briefly log visitors’ IP addresses for security: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The buttons to the Facebook page, Messenger and WhatsApp open Meta’s websites, where Meta’s privacy policy applies. Our site does not load anything from Facebook.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise them, write to us at contact@icoane.biz. You may also lodge a complaint with the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP): https://www.dataprotection.ro
